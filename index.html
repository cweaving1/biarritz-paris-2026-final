Before you go

The time
9:54 AM is French local time (CEST). SNCF Connect never converts to your timezone — this is the time on the ticket and the platform board. France is 8 hours ahead of Denver on this date.
Getting there
Biarritz-La Négresse station is about 3 km from the town centre, a €15–25 taxi. Leave Le Garage by roughly 9:15.
Strikes
A recurring risk on French rail. SNCF publishes revised timetables 48–72 hours ahead if one lands — worth checking the week before.
Trainline
Its US site was showing these trains an hour off. Ignore it; SNCF Connect is the source of truth.

Weather

Late September highs around 70–72°F, lows in the mid-50s. September is the driest month on this coast.

Tipping

Service is included by law. Rounding up is normal; percentage tipping is not expected.

Practical

À savoir

Weather

Late September highs near 68°F, falling to about 54°F after dark. Rain is likely at some point.

Sunday

Many restaurants and most shops close on Sunday. Confirm hours before planning the last day.

Reservations

TheFork and Zenchef for most restaurants, direct for the rest. Books typically open two to four weeks ahead.

Transit

Navigo Easy loaded with rides, or a multi-day pass. Confirm current fares.
